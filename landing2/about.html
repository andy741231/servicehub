Our story

A steady presence
in a changing city.

Harbor Church began with a small group of neighbors who believed church could be both deeply rooted and genuinely open.

Faith that meets
you where you are.

We are a multi-generational community centered on Jesus and committed to the good of our neighbors. We believe belonging comes before having all the right words.

So we gather with intention, listen with humility, and serve with consistency. Our hope is to be a trustworthy presence in Your City — a place where people can ask honest questions and take a meaningful next step.
