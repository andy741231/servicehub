1. Introduction

The Church Directory is the church's online phone list. It lets the saints look one another up for fellowship, and it gives each district's serving ones a way to keep the records for their district accurate, complete, and secure.

This guide is for the brothers and sisters who serve the directory:

RoleWhat the role is for
SaintKeeps their own contact info current and looks up other saints for fellowship.
HelperAssists the saints in their district and the approvers; can add new saints (new records start as Pending).
ApproverEnsures the records in their district are accurate, complete, and secure; activates new saints after checking for duplicates; manages removals.
AdminMaintains the directory for all roles in all districts. Service Hub platform admins and super admins are automatically directory Admins.

Helpers and approvers can only perform management actions on records in their own district — the server enforces this, so there is no way to change another district's records by accident.

For the saints' side of the directory (signing in, browsing, updating their own profile, texting), see the companion Church Directory — Saints' Guide. This guide summarizes those basics where a serving one needs them to help a saint.

Where things are

Saints, helpers, and approvers use the directory website: https://houstonservicehub.azurewebsites.net/directory. Staff who have a Service Hub account (and all admins) can also work from the Hub: https://houstonservicehub.azurewebsites.net/hub-admin → sidebar "Directory". The text-message phone list is at 713-257-9799.

2. The Spirit of This Service

The directory is a service to the saints, not just a database. A few principles from the church's requirements shape how we use it:

  • Add in fellowship. Adding a saint should be done in fellowship with the saint and the responsible brothers in the district. Always check first that the person isn't already listed — perhaps by another helper or in another district.
  • One record per person. When a saint moves to another district, there should be fellowship between both districts, and the district is changed on the existing record — never create a second record.
  • Removal is a shepherding matter. Setting someone Not Active is done in fellowship, after prayer and shepherding — when they have expressed that they no longer intend to meet, or their contact information is no longer valid. If they are recovered, simply reactivate the record.
  • Records are almost never physically deleted. Records get a removal status that only Approvers and Admins can see. Only duplicate records may be physically deleted, and only after deciding which record has the most correct information.
  • Saints maintain their own data. Serving ones encourage and tutor the saints, and make changes for them only when needed — for example when a saint has no texting phone, has an aversion to technology, or is in ill health.
  • Protect the saints' information. Just because you can see information doesn't mean you should share it. See Section 17.
  • Problems go up the chain. Saints ask helpers or approvers first; if a problem can't be resolved there, the administrators help.

3. Signing In

3.1 On the directory website

Go to https://houstonservicehub.azurewebsites.net/directory. The sign-in page is titled "The Church in Houston Directory".

Default method — email link: enter your email in the "Your email in the directory" field and click "Send me a sign-in link". The link in the email works once and expires in 30 minutes. If the email doesn't arrive, check Junk/Spam. If you just requested one, you'll see "A link was just sent — check your inbox. You can request another in a minute."

Password sign-in: click "Sign in with a password instead" to use Email + Password. The "Stay signed in" checkbox is checked by default. A password only exists after the person has signed in once with an email link and then set a password on their Profile page (the "Sign-in options" card: "New password" + "Confirm password" → "Set password", minimum 8 characters). Setting a password signs out that person's other devices. If someone forgets their password, they simply sign in with the email link again and set a new one.

A signed-in session lasts up to 30 days. The "Sign out" button is at the top right.

The bottom of the sign-in page lists the helpers and approvers (name, district, and "approver" where applicable) whom a saint can contact for help — useful when coaching a saint who can't get in.

Important

Only Active records can sign in. If a record's status is changed away from Active, that person is signed out immediately. A Pending, Inactive, or removed saint cannot sign in until an approver activates (or reactivates) their record.

3.2 By text message

Text ME to 713-257-9799 from the phone number on your record (Phone 1). You receive a link by text: "HI JOHN, use this link to REVIEW or UPDATE your information:". This is often the easiest way to help a saint who doesn't use email.

3.3 Through the Service Hub (staff & admins)

Staff who have a Service Hub account sign in at https://houstonservicehub.azurewebsites.net/hub-admin and open "Directory" in the sidebar, which shows "Dashboard", "Browse", and "My Profile". Hub access to the Directory requires the Hub "Directory" app permission and a linked directory record whose status is Active — your directory role comes from that linked record. Hub platform admins are directory Admins everywhere. Linking a Hub login to a directory record is an Admin-only task.

Helpers and approvers who don't have a Hub account simply use the saints' website — they get the same management tools there (see Section 6).

4. What You Can See

What a record shows you depends on your role and on whether the record is in your district:

RoleRecords visibleDetail level
Saint Only Active saints who are listed (opted in), church-wide. Fields masked by each saint's privacy choices ("Hidden" / "Not provided").
Helper All Active listed saints church-wide, plus every record in their own district except removed statuses (Moved, Deceased, Negative, Duplicate, Delete). Same privacy masking as saints for church-wide records; full unmasked details for records in their own district.
Approver All Active listed saints church-wide, plus all statuses in their own district. Full unmasked details for records they manage (their district).
Admin Everything, every district, every status. Full details everywhere.

Once a helper sets a record to a removed status (e.g. Moved), it disappears from the helper's view — approvers and admins can still see it.

5. The Dashboard

The Dashboard is available in the Service Hub (/hub-admin → "Directory" → "Dashboard"). It gives a quick picture of the directory:

  • "Active Saints" — total count, with "N districts represented".
  • "My District" — your district and your role.
  • "Pending" — records "awaiting activation".
  • "Quick Actions" — "Browse", "My Profile", "Add Member" (helpers and above), and "Review Pending" (approvers/admins; opens the member table filtered to Pending).
  • A "Pending activation" list (or "Recently added" when there is nothing pending), a "Browse Directory" button, and a "Districts — Active saints by district" chart.
Tip

Approvers: make the "Review Pending" quick action your first stop each day — pending saints can't sign in or be seen by anyone until you activate them.

6. Browsing & the Member Table

On "Browse", staff default to the "Table" view; a "Cards"/"Table" toggle switches to the same card view the saints see. Staff also get extra controls: an "All statuses" filter, "Refresh", and "Add Member". Below the toolbar a line reads "N members · management scoped to <your district>" (not shown to admins, who manage all districts).

The table

  • Columns: Name, District, Status, Role, Phone, Email, Added. Click a name to open the member's details.
  • Pages of 50 rows with "Previous"/"Next".
  • Row actions:
    • A green "Activate" button (approvers/admins) on Pending or Inactive rows.
    • A "⋮" menu with "View details", "Edit", "Set status" (options depend on your role — see Section 11), "Set role" (see Section 13), and "Delete permanently" (only on Duplicate records, approvers/admins only).
  • Every status or role change asks for confirmation.

Searching and filtering

The search box ("Search by name, email, or phone…") and the "Last name A–Z" dropdown work the same as for saints. Staff additionally get the "All statuses" filter and the district buttons ("All" plus the 12 districts; several may be selected).

7. Viewing a Member's Details

Click a member's name (table) or card to open the details dialog. It shows contact info, locality, spouse, the date the record was added, date of birth, and head of household.

For staff, an extra box shows:

  • Marital status and gender;
  • Privacy: whether the phone and address are visible or hidden, and whether the saint is opted in or out of the listing;
  • Provenance: "Imported from the 2024 phone list" or "Added by staff", plus "verified <date>" or "not yet verified" with a "Mark verified" button;
  • "Last <change> by <name> on <date>" — the most recent change;
  • A "History" list of the latest 50 changes (date, who, what).

An "Edit" button opens the edit form (Section 10).

Tip

Imported records stay "not yet verified" until a serving one confirms the details and clicks "Mark verified" (any staff edit also marks the record verified). When you contact a saint and confirm their info, take the extra second to verify the record.

8. Adding a New Saint

Helpers, approvers, and admins can add saints — but only into their own district (unless admin). Every new record starts as Pending: it is not visible to the saints and cannot sign in until an approver activates it.

Step 0 — Fellowship first

Adding should be done in fellowship with the saint and the responsible brothers in the district. Confirm the person isn't already listed before you start.

Step 1 — "Check for an existing record"

Click "Add Member" (on Browse or the Dashboard). Helpers, approvers, and admins can also text ADD to the directory number — the reply is a one-time sign-in link that opens this same form. The first step searches the whole church — all districts, any status — by name, email, or phone (at least 2 characters).

  • Results show name · district · email/phone and status.
  • Warnings appear as needed: "Likely the same person — confirm this is really someone new:" with notes like "— same phone" or "— same name", or "Possible existing records:".
  • "No matches found — safe to add." means you're clear to proceed.
  • The "Continue to add" button stays disabled until a search has finished.
Important

If the person already exists — even in another district, or with status Inactive — do not add a second record. Fellowship instead, and have an approver change the district (Section 10) or reactivate the record (Section 11).

Step 2 — The form

Required fields: "First name *", "Last name *", and "District *" (defaults to your district; only approvers/admins can change it — helpers see "Only approvers/admins can change district").

Optional fields: Middle name, Other name, Email (needed for email sign-in; must be unique — "A member with this email already exists"), Gender (brother/sister), Marital status, Date of birth, Locality (default Houston), Small group, Phone 1 (needed for text-message access), Phone 2, Street address, Apt #, City, State (default TX), ZIP, "Spouse (linked member)" (search-and-link, or spouse first/last name as free text), and the checkboxes "Phone visible to other saints" (default on), "Address visible to other saints" (default off), "Listed in the directory" (default on), and "Head of household".

Click "Add member". Remind the saint their record is Pending until an approver activates it — then they can sign in by email link or by texting ME.

Tip

Enter Phone 1 carefully — the text-message service recognizes a saint by the Phone 1 on their record. If it changes later, update it so texting keeps working.

9. Activating New Saints (Approvers)

Approvers and admins activate Pending records. Find them via the Dashboard's "Review Pending" quick action, or Browse → Table → status filter "Pending".

  1. Before activating, verify there is no duplicate — use the Add Member duplicate search or the Browse search.
  2. Click the green "Activate" button on the row.
  3. Confirm the dialog: "Activating makes this saint visible to everyone in the directory. Verify there is no duplicate record first."

Once Active, the saint can sign in (email link or text ME) and appears to everyone in the directory.

Couples

Add both spouses, activate both, then link them via Edit → "Spouse (linked member)". Each spouse keeps their own record and sign-in.

10. Editing Records & Helping Saints Update

Editing as staff

  • Helpers and approvers can edit records in their own district (helpers cannot edit removed records).
  • Changing a record's district requires approver or admin — "District changes require approver role or above". A move between districts is an edit, never a new record.
  • Changing the email also changes the email the person signs in with. If another sign-in already uses it you'll see "Another directory sign-in already uses that email".
  • If a saint's cell number changes, update Phone 1 so the text service still recognizes them.
  • Staff may upload a photo for members they manage.
  • Every change is recorded in History with who made it.

Helping a saint update their own info

Saints maintain their own data on the "Profile" tab — serving ones encourage and tutor them, and only edit for them when needed (no texting phone, technology aversion, ill health). The saints' guide has the full details; in brief:

  1. Have them sign in (email link, or text ME to 713-257-9799).
  2. Open the "Profile" tab — editable fields include Email, Other name, Phone 1, Phone 2, street address fields, marital status, date of birth, small group, spouse names, and the privacy checkboxes.
  3. "Save changes" lights up only after something changes; success shows "Saved — your info is up to date."
  4. Their name, district, status, and role are maintained by the district's helpers and approvers — they contact you to change those.

Reactivating

If a saint is recovered, an approver or admin clicks "Activate" on the Inactive row — the record is Active again, visible to the saints, and the person can sign in.

Privacy

Setting someone Inactive — or any removed status — is a shepherding matter done in fellowship, after prayer, when they have expressed that they no longer intend to meet or their contact information is no longer valid. It is not a bookkeeping shortcut.

12. Handling Duplicates

Two records for the same person should be merged, not left side by side:

  1. Decide which record has the most correct information — that one is kept.
  2. Edit the kept record to merge in any correct details from the other (phones, address, email, spouse link, etc.).
  3. Set the other record's status to Duplicate (approver; "⋮" → "Set status").
  4. An approver or admin may then choose "Delete permanently" from the "⋮" menu — available only for Duplicate records. The confirmation warns: "Only duplicate records may be physically deleted. This cannot be undone."
Important

Physical deletion removes the record's sign-in account and its history, and cannot be undone. When in doubt, leave the record as Duplicate — it stays invisible to everyone except approvers and admins.

14. Daily Email Digest

Every morning at 6:00 AM Central, helpers and approvers who have an email on file receive one email for their district containing:

  • the previous day's changes to the district's records, and
  • pending members re-listed every 3rd day they remain pending — a reminder to activate them.

If there is nothing to report, no email is sent.

Tip

Make sure your own record has a working email address, or you won't receive the digest.

15. Using Text Messages as a Serving One

Serving ones use the same text number as the saints: 713-257-9799, texted from the phone number that is Phone 1 on your Active record. Searching (john, last smith, john smith), ME, LOCKUP, STOP/START, and the reply footer all work the same way — see the Saints' Guide, Section 9, for the full command list.

Replying GET HELP shows a helper or approver menu with staff topics in addition to the saints' topics. Numbered replies only work for 10 minutes after the menu appears.

Important — retired text commands

The older staff text commands (SET …, SETSRV …, HIM/HER, ADDRESS, PIC, LIST, SEND, ATTENDANCE) are retired. Texting one replies "This moved to the web: https://houstonservicehub.azurewebsites.net/directory". Some GET HELP topics still mention those old commands — use the website steps in this guide instead.

ADD is the exception: texting it replies with a one-time sign-in link that opens the Add Member form on the website (Section 8).

If the service is paused for maintenance you may receive "System is in test for a few minutes." — try again shortly.

16. For Administrators

Admins sign in through the Service Hub (https://houstonservicehub.azurewebsites.net/hub-admin → "Directory"). Service Hub platform admins and super admins are automatically directory Admins.

  • See and manage all districts — no scope line on Browse.
  • Set any status, including Pending (sends a record back for review) and Delete; use "Delete permanently" for duplicate records.
  • Set any role — appoint helpers and approvers via "Set role".
  • Grant a Hub user Directory access in Service Hub user management (the "Directory" app permission). Their directory role comes from their linked directory record, and the record must be Active — linking a Hub login to a directory record is an Admin-only task.
  • Background jobs run automatically: nightly maintenance at 3:30 AM Central and the district digest at 6:00 AM Central.
  • Help the saints, helpers, and approvers when problems can't be resolved at the district level.

17. Protecting Privacy

Privacy

Just because you can see information doesn't mean you should share it. Protecting the saints' information comes before fulfilling a request.

  • Serving ones see more than the saints do — full phone numbers and addresses for the district's records, including fields the saint chose to hide. Treat that access as a trust.
  • If someone asks for a list of addresses, phone numbers, or similar — even for a seemingly good purpose — seek fellowship with a responsible brother first before sharing anything.
  • Respect each saint's privacy choices: "Show my phone number to other saints" (default on), "Show my address to other saints" (default off), and "List me in the directory". A saint who unchecked "List me in the directory" is still visible to staff — that doesn't mean their info may be passed around.
  • Email addresses are always visible to signed-in saints; be aware of this when helping someone choose what to share.

18. Troubleshooting

Website sign-in messages

MessageWhat it means / what to do
"We couldn't find a directory record for that email. Check the spelling or contact a directory helper."That email is not on any record. Check spelling; the record may use a different email — search for the person and check their record's email.
"This directory record is not currently active. Contact a helper or approver in your district."The record is Pending, Inactive, or removed. An approver should review and activate/reactivate if appropriate.
"This sign-in has been disabled. Contact a directory administrator."The person's sign-in account was disabled — refer to an admin.
"That sign-in link is invalid or has expired. Request a new one."Links work once and expire in 30 minutes. Request a fresh link.
"No password account yet — sign in with the email link first, then set a password from My Profile."They tried password sign-in before ever setting a password. Use the email link first.
"Invalid email or password"Wrong email/password combination. If forgotten, sign in with the email link and set a new password.
"A link was just sent — check your inbox. You can request another in a minute."A link was already requested; wait about a minute before asking for another. Check Junk/Spam.

Text-message replies

ReplyWhat it means / what to do

"System is in test for a few minutes."The text service is paused; try again shortly.
"This moved to the web: https://houstonservicehub.azurewebsites.net/directory"A retired staff text command was used. Do the task on the website instead (Section 15). ADD is the exception — it replies with a sign-in link that opens the Add Member form.
"Click this link to input information for a new saint: …"A reply to ADD. Tap the link to sign in and open the Add Member form. The link works once and expires in 30 minutes.

Escalation path

Saints ask their district's helpers or approvers first. If a problem can't be resolved there, contact an administrator.
