1. Welcome

The Church Directory is the church's phone list on the internet. It helps you find the saints' phone numbers and email addresses so you can get in touch for fellowship — and it lets you keep your own contact information up to date so the saints can reach you.

You do not need to install anything. It works in a web browser on your phone, tablet, or computer — and it also works by plain text message if you prefer not to use the website at all.

Important

The directory is only for fellowship within the church. Please don't share the saints' contact information with anyone outside the church, and don't use it for business or other purposes.

2. Two Ways to Use the Phone List

You can use the directory whichever way is most comfortable for you:

WayHowGood for
The website Go to https://houstonservicehub.azurewebsites.net/directory in your browser. Browsing the list, seeing photos, updating all your information, choosing your privacy settings, adding your photo.
Text messages Send a text to 713-257-9799 from your own cell phone. Quickly looking up a phone number, or getting a sign-in link, without opening a browser.

Both ways show the same up-to-date information. The rest of this guide covers the website first, then texting (Section 9).

3. Signing In on the Website

Open your browser and go to https://houstonservicehub.azurewebsites.net/directory. You will see the sign-in page, "The Church in Houston Directory". There are three ways to sign in.

3.1 Sign in with an email link (the usual way)

  1. Type your email address — the one the church has on your record — into "Your email in the directory".
  2. Click "Send me a sign-in link".
  3. Open your email and click the link in the message. That's it — you're signed in.
  • The link works once and expires after 30 minutes, so click it soon.
  • If you don't see the email, look in your Junk or Spam folder.
  • If you request a link twice in a row you'll see "A link was just sent — check your inbox. You can request another in a minute." Wait a moment and check your inbox.
Tip

If no email arrives after a few minutes, ask a helper or approver in your district which email address is on your record — the sign-in page lists them at the bottom.

3.2 Sign in with a password

If you'd rather type a password than wait for an email, click "Sign in with a password instead", then enter your Email and Password. The "Stay signed in" box is checked for you, so you won't need to sign in every time.

You can only use a password after you have signed in once with an email link and then created a password:

  1. Sign in with the email link.
  2. Open the "Profile" tab and find the "Sign-in options" card.
  3. Enter a "New password" and "Confirm password" (at least 8 characters), then click "Set password".

If you forget your password, just sign in with the email link and set a new one. (Setting a password signs you out on your other devices.)

3.3 Sign in by text message

No email? Send a text that just says ME to 713-257-9799 from the cell phone number the church has on your record. You'll get a text back with a link — "HI JOHN, use this link to REVIEW or UPDATE your information:" — tap it to open your page already signed in.

Staying signed in, and signing out

A sign-in lasts up to 30 days, so most of the time you can just open the website and be in already. To sign out (for example on a shared computer), click "Sign out" at the top right.

Once you're in, you'll see two tabs at the top: "Browse" (the list of saints) and "Profile" (your own information).

4. Finding Saints

Open the "Browse" tab. You'll see the "Church Directory" page with the saints' cards. Here's how to find someone:

  • Search box — type part of a name, email, or phone into "Search by name, email, or phone…".
  • "Last name A–Z" — pick a letter to jump to surnames starting with it.
  • District buttons — "All" plus the twelve districts (Central 1, Central 2, Central 3, C - Sugar Land, C - Diho, C - Medical Ctr, S - Spanish Lang, Southwest, South, Southeast, North, West). Tap one or more to narrow the list.

Just above the cards you'll see "Showing X of Y saints", and at the bottom a "Load more (N remaining)" button to see the rest.

What's on each card

Each saint's card shows their photo (or initials), name, district and small group, email (tap it to write an email), phone (tap it to call), city and state, and "Married to …" where that applies. Helpers and approvers show a role badge on their card so you know who can help you.

Tap a card to see the full details. In the details:

  • "Hidden" means that saint chose to keep that information private.
  • "Not provided" simply means no information is on file yet.
Tip

Only saints whose records are active and who have chosen to be listed appear in the directory. If you can't find someone, they may be unlisted or their record may not be active — see the FAQ.

5. Keeping Your Information Up to Date

Open the "Profile" tab. At the top you'll see your name, status, role badge (if any), and your district · small group.

A note on the page explains: "Your name, district, status, and role are maintained by your district's helpers and approvers — contact them to change those." Everything else you can update yourself:

  • Email
  • Other name (maiden name or non-English name)
  • Phone 1 and Phone 2, and "Show my phone number to other saints"
  • Street address, Apt #, City, State, ZIP, and "Show my address to other saints"
  • Marital status (single / married / widowed / divorced) and Date of birth
  • Small group
  • Spouse first/last name (only when your spouse doesn't have a record linked to yours)
  • "List me in the directory" — uncheck to hide yourself (see Section 6)

Click "Save changes" when you're done — the button becomes clickable only after you've changed something, and you'll see "Saved — your info is up to date."

Important

Phone 1 is the number the text-message service knows you by. If you get a new cell number, update Phone 1 (or ask a helper) or texting 713-257-9799 will no longer recognize you.

Tip

Changing your email also changes the email you sign in with — use an address you can still read mail at.

If something important is missing, a yellow note at the top of the page will say so — "Your record is missing … — please fill it in so the saints can reach you." If you'd like a hand, the footer on the Browse page says "Need help updating your info? Contact a helper or approver in your district (…) — they show a badge on their card."

6. Your Privacy Choices

You control how much other signed-in saints see. Here is exactly what is shown:

InformationWho sees itYour choice
Email Always visible to other signed-in saints — there is no hide option for email. None — choose an email you're comfortable sharing with the church.
Phone numbers Saints see them unless you turn this off. "Show my phone number to other saints" — on by default.
Home address Saints don't see it unless you turn this on. "Show my address to other saints" — off by default.
Being listed at all You appear in Browse and in text-message results. "List me in the directory" — "Uncheck to hide yourself from other saints (helpers/approvers can still see you)".

Whatever you hide is simply shown as "Hidden" to other saints — it is never deleted, and the helpers and approvers in your district can still see it so they can reach you when needed.

Privacy

Please treat other saints' information the same way. If anyone asks you for a list of addresses or phone numbers — even for a good cause — please check with a responsible brother first rather than passing it on.

7. Your Household

If your spouse has a record in the directory that is linked to yours, your Profile page shows a "Household" card. From your own page you can update your spouse's:

  • phone numbers,
  • address,
  • privacy choices (phone/address visibility),
  • small group,
  • date of birth, other name, and marital status.

Their name, district, status, and sign-in email are maintained by the district's helpers and approvers — contact them for those changes.

If your spouse is not in the directory (or their record isn't linked), you can simply type their first and last name into the spouse fields on your own profile.

8. Your Photo

A photo helps the saints recognize you. On your Profile page, click the small camera button on your picture ("Change photo") and choose a picture from your device.

  • JPG, PNG, GIF, or WebP formats are fine.
  • Up to 5 MB in size — most phone photos work.

If you don't have a photo, your initials show in its place — that's fine too.

9. Using Text Messages

You can use the whole phone list by ordinary text message — no sign-in, no app. Send your texts to 713-257-9799.

Important

You must text from the cell number that is Phone 1 on your record. If you text from a different number you'll get back "Your phone number not recognized. To be added to the phone list, contact a helper in your district."

The first time you text, the reply includes a welcome: "Welcome to the secure, online phone list! …" with a few practice suggestions.

Looking someone up

Just type a name — there's no special command word:

You textWhat it does
johnSearches first names for "John". If nothing is found, it automatically tries last names, then a partial match.
last smithSearches last names for "Smith".
john smithSearches the full name.

It isn't case sensitive, and nicknames work too — "Christopher (Chris)" can be found by texting chris.

A typical reply looks like:

John Smith, 713-555-1234, SW

That's the name, phone number, and district code. For a couple you'll see:

John & Mary Smith, SW John, 713-555-1234 Mary, 713-555-9876

If someone has chosen to hide their phone number, it is left out of the results. When a search matches many people, results are limited to 12 ("--- results limited to 12") — just add more of the name to narrow it down.

All the commands

You textWhat happens
a name (e.g. john smith)Looks the person up and texts back their phone number.
MESends you a link to review or update your own information. If several records share your phone (like a couple), it asks "Which record? Reply with a number:" — reply 1, 2…, or 0 to cancel.
GET HELP (or GETHELP)Sends a numbered menu of help topics — reply with a topic number within 10 minutes.
LOCKUPSends a link to the lockup procedures and schedule.
STOPStops all texts to your phone. (Also STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT.)
STARTTurns the texts back on after a STOP. (Also UNSTOP, YES.)

The GET HELP menu topics for saints are:

  1. how to search,
  2. how to update your info (text ME),
  3. if your cell number changes (ask a helper),
  4. list the helpers in your district (approvers are marked with *),
  5. the goals of the phone list.

Every reply ends with "Text GET HELP for info, or text STOP to unsubscribe". Long answers may arrive as several texts. If a numbered reply says "… is not valid -- you don't have a command active", the 10-minute window has passed — text GET HELP again. And if you get "System is in test for a few minutes.", the service is paused briefly — try again in a little while.

10. Getting Help

If anything doesn't work or you're not sure what to do, ask a helper or approver in your district — that's what they're there for. Ways to reach them:

  • On the sign-in page — the bottom of the page lists the helpers and approvers (name, district, and "approver"). You can see this even before signing in.
  • In Browse — helpers and approvers have a badge on their card, so you can spot them and tap their card for their phone or email.
  • By text — send GET HELP to 713-257-9799 and choose topic 4 to list the helpers in your district.

If a helper or approver can't solve it, they'll pass it to the directory administrators.

11. Frequently Asked Questions

I requested a sign-in link but no email arrived.

Check your Junk or Spam folder first — the link email often lands there the first time. Also wait about a minute; if you clicked "Send me a sign-in link" twice, the page will tell you a link was already sent. Still nothing? The email on your record may be old or misspelled — contact a helper or approver in your district (listed at the bottom of the sign-in page) to check it.

The link says it's invalid or expired.

Sign-in links work only once and expire after 30 minutes. Just request a new one from the sign-in page.

It says "We couldn't find a directory record for that email."

The email you typed isn't on any record. Check the spelling, try another email you use, or ask a helper which email is on your record.

It says my record "is not currently active."

Your record is pending, inactive, or otherwise not active yet. Contact a helper or approver in your district — an approver can activate it.

My cell number changed. What do I do?

Update Phone 1 on your Profile page (or ask a helper to do it). The text-message service recognizes you by Phone 1, so texting won't work until it's updated.

How do I change my name or district?

Those fields are maintained by your district's helpers and approvers — just ask one of them. The note at the top of your Profile page explains this.

Why can't I find someone in the directory?

A few possibilities: their record may not be active yet (new records need an approver to activate them), they may have unchecked "List me in the directory", or the spelling you're searching may differ from their record. Try the "Last name A–Z" dropdown or a shorter part of the name. A helper can check whether they're listed.

I don't want my phone number or address shown.

On your Profile page, uncheck "Show my phone number to other saints" and leave "Show my address to other saints" unchecked (it starts hidden). To disappear from the listing entirely, uncheck "List me in the directory". Remember that your email is always visible to signed-in saints.

Someone asked me for a list of addresses or phone numbers.

Please don't send it. Check with a responsible brother first — protecting the saints' information matters more than filling a request quickly.

Can I use the directory without a computer or email?

Yes — text the phone list at 713-257-9799 from your cell phone (Section 9). Text ME for a link to review your info, or just type a name to look someone up.

My spouse's information is wrong but I can't edit it all.

If your spouse's record is linked to yours, the "Household" card on your Profile lets you update their phones, address, privacy choices, small group, date of birth, other name, and marital status. Their name, district, status, and sign-in email are changed by the helpers or approvers.
