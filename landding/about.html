Our story

A steady place
in a moving city.

Harbor Church began with a borrowed room, a long table, and a conviction that church should feel more like home than a performance.

We’re learning
to live open.

We are a multi-generational community centered on Jesus and curious about what it looks like to love our neighbors in practical ways.

That means we show up: for Sunday worship, for hard conversations, for meals, for the block, and for one another. Nobody has to have it all together before they come through the door.

Come meet us ↗
01 / WELCOME

Make room.

Every person is made in the image of God. We want our spaces, language, and tables to reflect that.

02 / PRESENCE

Be here.

We choose attention over hurry. We practice listening, showing up, and staying connected.

03 / ACTION

Do good.

Hope is not abstract. We partner with local organizations to care for our city in tangible ways.
