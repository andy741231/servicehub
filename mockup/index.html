A little about me

About & Philosophy

I'm a product designer and front-end developer based in Portland. I work at the intersection of product thinking and UI engineering, with a bias toward systems that stay simple as they scale.

My process is rooted in research, prototyping, and close collaboration. I believe the best interfaces are the ones people don't have to think about — clear hierarchy, consistent patterns, and motion that guides rather than distracts.

Clarity first Every element should earn its place. I reduce noise until the message is unavoidable.
Systems thinking I build reusable components and tokens so the product stays coherent as it grows.
Accessible by default Great design works for everyone. I design with contrast, keyboard flows, and screen readers in mind.
Motion with meaning Transitions are punctuation, not decoration. They guide attention and reinforce hierarchy.
